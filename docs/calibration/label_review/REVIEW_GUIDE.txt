HUMAN REVIEW
1. Give reviewers only outputs/form_label_review/ when available. It contains
   shuffled numbered copies, reviewer_index.csv, and reviewer_rep_annotations.csv.
   Keep data/raw/mendeley/review_id_mapping.csv separate until review is complete.
   The original side_view_review_queue.csv is a locator for the dataset organizer;
   its filenames expose source good/bad labels, so it is NOT a blind review list.
   Hide app predictions too.
2. A trainer or suitably qualified movement reviewer defines the exercise rubric
   before reviewing. Record their qualification; ordinary volunteer labels must
   not be described as expert ground truth.
3. Add one row per complete rep to rep_annotations.csv. Use start_seconds and
   end_seconds relative to the ORIGINAL video. Define a squat rep as standing,
   descent, bottom, return to standing; a push-up as top, descent, bottom, return
   to top. Exclude incomplete edge segments or describe them separately.
4. assessable is yes/no. Missing joints, occlusion, unsuitable view, ambiguous
   boundaries or insufficient visual evidence should receive no, with a reason.
   Tracking failure is not evidence of incorrect form.
5. overall_form is correct/incorrect/uncertain/unassessable under the written
   reviewer rubric. Each fault is present/absent/uncertain/not_applicable.
   Leave these fields BLANK until someone actually reviews the rep.
6. Record visible evidence in evidence_notes. Do not use the app's angle
   thresholds to manufacture reference labels. Whole-clip good/bad labels must
   never be copied to every rep or converted into a specific fault label.
7. Have a second reviewer independently annotate a subset (preferably all final
   test reps). Preserve their separate original worksheet. Resolve disagreements
   explicitly and record adjudication_status as agreed/resolved/unresolved.

FAULT RUBRIC TO FINALIZE WITH THE REVIEWER
limited_depth: observed movement does not reach the agreed exercise/participant
target range. Document the target and exercise variant before assigning labels.
incomplete_extension: the rep does not return to the agreed top position.
trunk_alignment_issue: visible deviation from the agreed exercise-specific
alignment. A side-view projected angle alone cannot establish spinal curvature,
injury risk, or every alignment fault. Use uncertain when the view cannot tell.
These are annotation categories, not medical thresholds or validated coaching.

NEW LAPTOP RECORDINGS
new_recording_plan.csv contains 48 EMPTY recording slots for 8 NEW participants,
2 exercises and 3 takes per exercise, targeting 3 complete reps per take.
This is a small pilot, not a statistically sufficient reliability claim.
All takes and future views from a person must stay in that person's split:
4 development participants, 2 validation participants, 2 final-test participants.
Obtain consent and keep videos under ignored data/raw/, with anonymous IDs.
Record naturally occurring variation in comfortable, unweighted movement; do not
ask people to force dangerous technique merely to create an incorrect example.
If both labels/fault outcomes are not represented, report the missing coverage
and collect more independently reviewed recordings before claiming validation.
No condition or filename preassigns the eventual form label.

EVALUATION
Existing Mendeley participants have prior development/test exposure. Use them
for development and regression; do not describe them as a new untouched test.
New boundaries/fault annotations on old participants do not erase that exposure.
Freeze annotation rubric, split, code, pose weights and thresholds before final
test inference. Keep final-test labels hidden during development. If tuning uses
final-test results, reserve another independent test before the next claim.
Evaluate one result per rep for each fault and overall form separately. Report
participant/rep counts, class counts, confusion matrices, per-fault precision and
recall, macro F1, abstentions and assessable coverage. Include unassessable cases;
report accuracy over assessable cases and over all cases separately. Report rep
boundary/count matching separately so missed or duplicated reps are not hidden.
Whole-session validation does not establish per-rep or live feedback accuracy.
